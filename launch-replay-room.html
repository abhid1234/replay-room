Product & launch brief · October 25, 2026

Replay Room

A safety system for the moment a failed webhook needs to be sent again.

In simple words: Replay Room is a flight recorder and rehearsal room for webhooks. It shows what failed, tests the exact recovery safely, requires evidence before a risky replay, and preserves an audit trail afterward.

01 · Why it matters

“Try it again” can charge someone twice.

The queue knows it failed

It often cannot tell whether the receiving system acted before the timeout, so a retry can duplicate a payment, shipment, or account change.

Most tools make resend easy

They do not prove that the payload and destination are unchanged, that a safe rehearsal passed, or that a second person approved a high-risk action.

Replay Room makes proof the gate

It binds the rehearsal, payload hash, target, operator identity, reason, risk acknowledgement, and approval into one explainable decision.

02 · How it works

From failed event to controlled recovery.

1. ReceivePersist the original event before acknowledging it.
2. DeliverSend asynchronously with bounded retries.
3. Dead letterKeep the full attempt transcript when retries end.
4. DiagnoseExplain outage, rate limit, contract, or network failure.
5. RehearseTest the exact payload against a controlled destination.
6. GuardCheck nine conditions and require dual control when risk is high.
7. ReplaySend once through a durable intent and export signed evidence.
03 · Architecture

Render runs the product. Postgres remembers the truth.

Static siteReact operator console and interactive outage drill.
Web serviceFastify API plus embedded BullMQ worker and reconciler on the free lab.
PostgresDurable events, delivery intents, attempts, rehearsals, approvals, and audit history.
Key ValueDisposable queue transport, delayed retries, rate limits, and heartbeats.
Hugging FaceCredential-free incident drill plus deterministic synthetic fixtures.
npm + GitHubPortable evidence verifier, schema, release tarball, SBOM, and provenance.
Redis can disappear. The operator should still be able to reconstruct exactly what must happen next.
Design principle: durable intent before queue dispatch
